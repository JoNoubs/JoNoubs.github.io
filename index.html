How I work

Four habits behind every project.

The same method, whether it is a company assistant or a weekend side project.

01

I start from one real person.

A medical student, future math teachers, a restaurant team. I build for their need, not for the technology.

02

I measure before I claim.

A set of real questions before saying "it works", and every answer traced in Langfuse, so I know why it is right or wrong.

03

I ship small, with tests.

Almost daily TestFlight builds, protected by 350+ UI tests.

04

I write my decisions down.

Every important choice is recorded with its reason: 18 architecture decisions documented for Mnésie, and LLM wikis so teams keep the memory of their choices.
